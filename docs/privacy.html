Domin-Uno Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

Domin-Uno is a scorekeeper for Mexican Train Dominoes. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. The app makes no network connections.
  • Scores and saved matches (player names and totals) are stored only on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.
  • Camera. When you touch and hold a player's points field, Domin-Uno uses the camera to photograph that player's tiles and count the pips. The photo is analyzed on your device, is not saved, and never leaves your device.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/spurious-cox/dominuno.
